Accident Prevention & Response Programs

Driver Certification

Defensive Driving Certification

A psychological and practical methodology that trains drivers to expect the unexpected and maintain safe escape routes.

Why it matters: Directly slashes collision rates, protects corporate vehicles, and drastically lowers insurance premiums.

Details: Visual scanning rules, wet-weather driving physics, and understanding blind spots of commercial and heavy fleet vehicles.

Logistics Control

Journey Management & Route Planning

Strategic preparation prior to driving, ensuring an authorized route, adequate rest stops, and monitored vehicle checks.

Why it matters: Eliminates incidents caused by extreme driver fatigue or preventable vehicular breakdown.

Details: Training dispatchers and drivers to use logbooks, pre-trip vehicle checklists (T-CLOCS), and fatigue evaluation standards.

Incident Protocol

Post-Incident Response & Reporting

The immediate and secondary actions a driver must take if involved in a collision on corporate time.

Why it matters: Secures the scene to prevent secondary accidents, protects the driver legally, and ensures proper medical care.

Details: Securing the vehicle, deploying warning triangles, first aid escalation protocols, and interacting correctly with authorities.
