Fire Safety Training

Proactive Fire Prevention & Rapid Emergency Response

Protect Lives & Facility Assets

Workplace safety is non-negotiable. Our Fire Safety Training ensures that your employees are fully equipped to identify fire hazards, prevent outbreaks, and respond effectively in the event of an emergency.

Participants will learn the essential principles of fire behavior, the correct use of various fire extinguishers, evacuation procedures, and how to conduct routine hazard assessments.

By implementing our comprehensive fire safety protocols, you not only protect lives and property but also ensure strict adherence to national and international occupational safety regulations.

Fire Safety Core Programs

Fire Warden

Fire Marshal / Warden Certification

Specialized training for designated personnel in charge of steering fire evacuation protocols during emergencies.

Why it matters: Provides a clear chain of command during a crisis, ensuring panic-free, structured evacuations that save lives.

Details: Teaches personnel how to sweep buildings, assist vulnerable individuals, liaise with emergency services, and enforce daily fire safety rules.

Hands-on Drills

Basic Fire Prevention & Extinguishing

A foundational, hands-on workshop tailored for the general workforce covering combustion principles and early intervention.

Why it matters: Equips every single employee with the awareness needed to stop a spark from turning into a disaster.

Details: Live demonstrations using varying categories of extinguishers (Water, Foam, CO2, Dry Powder) and hazard spotting walkthroughs.

Facility Protocols

Emergency Evacuation & Drill Management

Design, implementation, and review of comprehensive facility-wide mock fire evacuation drills.

Why it matters: Identifies bottlenecks, ensuring assembly points and alarm systems function perfectly under real pressure.

Details: We script the drill, monitor the evacuation metrics (speed vs safety), debrief the management team, and modify the master fire plan.
